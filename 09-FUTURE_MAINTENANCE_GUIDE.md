# 08. User & Admin Manual

## 📖 User Operating Manual

### 🛒 1. How to Process a Sales Invoice (POS / New Order)
1. Click the green **"+ New Sale Order / POS"** button at the top header or in the **Sales Orders** module.
2. Select an existing **Customer** or click **"+ Quick Add"** to register a new customer.
3. Search products by Name or scan the **Barcode**.
4. Adjust item quantities and unit prices (note: price cannot drop below `Min Selling Price`).
5. Type custom name engraving or photo printing notes under **Item Remarks**.
6. Enter **Advance Paid** amount (e.g., bKash / Cash).
7. Click **"Save Order & Print Memo"**. The 80mm POS thermal print modal will automatically pop up.

---

### 📦 2. How to Receive Purchase Inventory
1. Go to **Purchases** ➔ Click **"+ New Purchase Order"**.
2. Select **Supplier** and type the Supplier's physical bill/memo number.
3. Select Products, quantities, and cost prices.
4. Input paid amount.
5. Click **"Save Purchase"**. Item stock (`currentStock`) will automatically update immediately.

---

## 🔑 Admin Operations Manual

### 1. User Account & Permission Management
1. Go to **User Roles** module.
2. Click **"+ Add New User"**.
3. Set Username, PIN / Password, Designation, and Role (`Admin`, `Manager`, or `Executive`).
4. Toggle specific module permissions (e.g. grant access to Sales & POS while hiding Financial Profit Reports).
5. Toggle **"Can Delete Records"** switch.

---

### 2. Viewing Real-Time Financial Profit Reports
1. Go to **Reports** ➔ **Profit & Loss Statement**.
2. Select Date Range (Today, This Week, Monthly, Custom).
3. Review **Sales Revenue**, **COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**, **Gross Margin**, and **Operating Net Profit**.
4. Click **"Print / Export PDF"** for executive accounting archives.
